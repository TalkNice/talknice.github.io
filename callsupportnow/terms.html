About the App

Call Support Now is an iOS app that provides a curated directory of customer service phone numbers for US companies. The app allows users to search for companies, view department-level contact numbers, and initiate calls or text messages directly from the app.

Free Trial

Call Support Now offers a 30-day free trial beginning on the date of first launch. During the trial, all features are available at no charge. When the trial expires, phone numbers are locked and a subscription is required to access them.

Subscription

After the free trial, continued access to phone numbers requires an active subscription:

  • Price: $2.99 per year
  • Billing: Charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase
  • Renewal: Automatically renews annually unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date
  • Cancellation: You may cancel at any time via iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Refunds: Subscription billing and refunds are managed by Apple per their standard App Store policies
